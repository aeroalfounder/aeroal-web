Baggage

Flexible baggage management from your booking

Passengers can add extra checked baggage directly from Manage Booking. The current flow is built around clear unit pricing and simple passenger-based controls.

  • Extra baggage is added per passenger.
  • Each additional bag currently follows the 23kg logic used by the booking interface.
  • Pricing updates automatically when baggage quantities change.
  • Support can be extended later with cabin-specific allowances and oversized baggage rules.
